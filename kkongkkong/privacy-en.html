Frozen Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Roomsixteen ("we") handles information in Frozen Match (Korean title 꽁꽁매치, the "Game"). It applies to the iPhone app on the App Store. If the Korean version of this policy differs from this translation, the Korean version prevails for users in Korea.

1. We do not collect personal information

The Game has no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email, and we do not run any server that stores user data.

The following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Game progress: coins, best score, boosters, tutorial progress and sound settings
  • A random ID generated on your device to keep your records apart. It cannot identify you.

This data is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Personalized ads and tracking

  1. When you first open the app, iOS asks whether to allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites. You can play the Game either way. If you don't allow it, you will see non-personalized ads.
  2. In regions where the law requires consent, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalized. The legal basis for personalized ads is your consent. Ads needed to keep the Game free are shown on the basis of legitimate interest.
  3. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  4. We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect advertising IDs ourselves.

4. International transfers

When ads load, the ad data in section 2 is sent over an encrypted connection to Google LLC in the United States and processed under Google's safeguards for international transfers.

5. Retention

We keep no personal information. Game data on your device is removed when you delete the app. Data held by Google or Apple is kept according to their policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data, you can delete your game data by deleting the app and withdraw ad consent as described in section 3. For data held by Google or Apple, contact them directly, or contact us and we will help.

7. Children

The Game is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child's data has been processed, please contact us.

8. Security

The Game is designed to avoid collecting personal data. Game data stays on your device, and all connections to third-party services use encryption (HTTPS).
